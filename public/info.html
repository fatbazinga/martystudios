shipping

Orders ship within 7-21 working days from either Athens, Greece or Frankfurt, Germany, with tracking.

Shipping is €8 per order, free over €150. Orders outside the EU may be charged import duties and taxes by your country on delivery.

returns

If you're in the EU, you have 14 days from delivery to withdraw from your purchase without giving a reason. Email us at customers@martyrstudios.store with your order number, then send the items back unworn and with tags within 14 days. We refund the full amount, including the original standard shipping cost, within 14 days of receiving the return, to the same payment method. Return shipping is paid by you unfortunatly.

privacy

This site has no accounts, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising cookies.

  • Your bag is saved only in your own browser (local storage) so it survives a refresh. We never see it until you check out.
  • When you pay, you enter your name, email, phone, shipping address and payment details on a payment page run by Stripe. We receive your name, contact details, address and what you ordered, so we can ship it. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Why we use it: to fulfil your order (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and to keep invoices for tax law (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • How long: order records are kept for as long as Greek tax law requires (normally [5] years), then deleted.
  • Who else processes it: Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (hosting and security; it processes IP addresses to block attacks), and the courier who delivers your parcel.
  • Your rights: you can ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it (where the law allows) by emailing [email]. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).
